


This is a really common trap: you styled the inner elements (.info-image, .image-caption) with fixed/max dimensions and assumed the outer containers would just
 "handle it." But nothing in your chain was actually fluid — you had a hardcoded width on the caption and an unreset default margin on figure sitting between your flex container 
 and its children. Flexbox centering math is only as good as the box sizes it's given; if a descendant is overflowing its flex item silently, no amount of justify-content
  or align-items will fix what you're seeing, because the problem isn't the centering logic — it's that the boxes being centered don't match what's rendering on screen.

Go check your DevTools box model on one of these <figure> elements right now — I'd bet money you'll see that 40px/40px margin sitting there, unaccounted for.
